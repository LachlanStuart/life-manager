# Assess effort against editable intention

Effort assessment uses the current editable intention and visible selection, rather than enforcing a frozen planning baseline. Intentions can reasonably change as tasks prove smaller than expected or new opportunities arise; the application relies on honest reassessment. Hiding a task therefore removes its weight from the current checklist denominator, while visible Skip and Cut tasks retain their weight without completion credit. Historical snapshots preserve earlier states for comparison without constraining current planning.
